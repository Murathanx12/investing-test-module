# PREREG — GRAND-ARENA-1 PHASE 1: known-answer worlds

**Registered 2026-08-12 UTC, BEFORE any learner cell was scored against the
final world specifications.** The worlds, the learners, the splitter and every
threshold in this document were committed in the same commit as this file, and
the sweep was run afterwards.

**Deflation budget: NOT CHARGED.** This registers no claim about any real
market and can never promote anything. It is an instrument test on synthetic
data, and the house rule that **synthetic performance is never alpha evidence**
is the reason it costs nothing and the reason it can never pay anything either.

---

## 1. The question

Before any learner is trusted to DISCOVER an exit rule, a regime, or a
specialist's reliability in real markets, it must first prove it can
REDISCOVER a rule planted by hand. A learner that cannot recover a known answer
has not earned the right to be believed when it reports an unknown one.

**This phase can fail, and a failure invalidates the interpretation of every
later phase.** That is what it is for.

## 2. What is planted

Twelve worlds, generated by `aegis_brain/arena/known_worlds.py`, each a monthly
panel of 200 names. Every world exposes the **identical 18-column feature
schema**, so the world's identity never leaks through what a learner can see.

| World | Planted mechanism | Correct answer |
|---|---|---|
| A | `f_mom` predicts forward returns, +10%/yr quintile spread | positive momentum loading, nothing else |
| B | `f_mom` mean-reverts, −10%/yr | NEGATIVE momentum loading |
| C | 2-state Markov regime (P stay 0.94): state 0 pays `f_mom`, state 1 pays `f_val`, ±14%/yr, state 1 also carries higher market vol | condition on the latent state |
| D | latent tension → P(bad month) 15%→55%; bad month costs −10%; observable precursor = tension + noise (AUC ≈ 0.73) | de-risk IN PROPORTION to estimated probability; never a peak call |
| E | `f_mom` pays +14%/yr ONLY where `f_rev > 0`, exactly zero elsewhere | recover the interaction, not an average tilt |
| F | `f_mom` pays +14%/yr where `f_val < 0.8` and −14%/yr above it | recover the THRESHOLD sign flip |
| G | `f_specA` pays +24%/yr inside `biotech` and nothing elsewhere; `f_specB` is noise | follow A in biotech only, claim nothing for B |
| H | `f_specB` pays +24%/yr inside `semis` and nothing elsewhere; `f_specA` is noise | mirror image of G |
| **I** | **nothing.** `f_mom` is made CONTEMPORANEOUSLY correlated with the same month's idiosyncratic return (ρ≈0.43) and has zero forward relationship | **NOTHING HERE** — and a large apparent effect indicates leakage |
| **J** | `f_rev` re-drawn fast (AR 0.15), +12%/yr GROSS, 65 bps one-way per leg against near-total monthly turnover | recover the gross edge, then **refuse to trade it** |
| K | stale names (`f_qual < −0.5`) earn −0.9%/mo, top-quality replacements +0.6%/mo, switching costs 30 bps, cash earns 0 and forgoes the premium | **REPLACE**, not cash |
| **L** | market returns i.i.d. with CONSTANT volatility; every market observable is noise | **no timing edge exists** |

The three worlds in bold are the negative controls. **A learner that "finds" an
edge there has failed, and that failure matters more than any success
elsewhere.**

`known_worlds.verify()` measures each plant against its own declared size and
the run **aborts** rather than scoring learners on a world that is not what it
claims.

## 3. Learners

`ridge` · `logistic` · `random_forest` · `lightgbm` · `mlp_torch` (32-16-1,
early-stopped on the temporally last 20% of train) · `evolutionary` (linear
genome, fitness = in-sample IC — the deliberate overfitting canary) ·
`hmm_regime` (2-state Gaussian HMM on market observables + a ridge per state,
FILTERED not smoothed) · `contextual_bandit` (LinUCB over
{follow A, follow B, follow momentum, no tilt} with sector context) ·
`offline_q` (conservative one-step fitted-Q with an explicit pessimism penalty).

Every scaler, every hyper-parameter and every feature transform is fitted
**inside the fold**.

## 4. Validation design

Expanding-window walk-forward, **8 folds, 96-month minimum train, 1-month purge
(the label horizon), 3-month embargo.** No random k-fold anywhere.

## 5. Decision rules — FROZEN

`MDE = 2.0 × max(HAC, IID) standard error` of the per-period series (CANON §19,
NIGHT-11 ruling). A result below its MDE is **NOT DETECTABLE** — never a kill,
never a pass.

| Label | Condition |
|---|---|
| **RECOVERED** | primary metric clears its MDE in the correct direction AND the world's mechanism probe passes |
| **PARTIAL** | primary clears its MDE but the mechanism probe fails |
| **MISSED** | primary does not clear its MDE (in a world where something was planted) |
| **FALSE-POSITIVE** | in a null world: primary clears its MDE in either direction. In a signal world: the model is detectably ANTI-predictive out of sample, or (world J) it declares a NET edge |
| **CORRECT-NULL** | in a null world: primary is inside its MDE |

Mechanism-probe thresholds, fixed here:

* a learner **uses** a feature when its out-of-sample score correlates with it
  cross-sectionally by ≥ **0.10** (Spearman, month-averaged);
* a **conditional** mechanism (interaction, threshold, sector) counts as
  recovered when reliance differs across the two sides of the condition by
  ≥ **0.15**;
* a **decoy** specialist is falsely claimed when reliance on it exceeds
  **0.10** anywhere;
* bandit: true-arm share inside the skilled sector minus elsewhere ≥ **0.25**,
  and no arm without skill above **0.45** in any sector (uniform = 0.25);
* world K: replacement chosen ≥ **0.25** more often on stale names, and cash
  ≤ **0.10** of all actions.

## 6. §20 batch self-check, pre-committed

82 cells will be scored. The batch is checked against itself: false positives in
null worlds are compared against the nominal 4.55% rate, and the **effective
number of distinct learners** is computed from the correlation of their
out-of-sample score vectors, because seven learners correlating at 0.9 are not
seven independent chances to find something.

## 7. Calibration disclosures — made before the sweep, from ORACLE calculations

Three worlds were re-specified after a power calculation showed that **no
policy, including a population-optimal one, could clear its own MDE**. A world
nobody can recover grades nothing, so this is a power fix and not a difficulty
fix. In every case the calculation used an oracle or population-form policy,
never a learner's result:

1. **D** — the first spec used a rare (12%) deep (−16%) shock; the
   population-form probabilistic policy came out at **1.46×** its MDE. Replaced
   with more frequent (≈20%), shallower (−10%) bad months and a 600-month
   sample; population-form policy now **1.98×**.
2. **L** — 600 months, so the negative control is a TIGHT null rather than an
   uninformative one.
3. **K** — two defects and one power parameter. Every slot was handed the *same*
   best outsider, so the replace leg of a month was one draw of idiosyncratic
   noise repeated forty times (MDE 14%/yr, larger than anything in the world);
   and the book was allowed to *evolve* under the random logging policy, which
   refreshed it until only 5% of logged states were stale against a population
   31%. Fixed: per-slot candidates, resampled book, 66 slots. The world's own
   optimal rule now sits at **2.97×** its MDE.

Also disclosed: the first LinUCB implementation used a unit-scale confidence
width against rewards of order 0.02, so its exploration bonus exceeded every
arm-value difference by 20× and it behaved as a uniform random policy while
reporting green. The width is now scaled by the running reward sd — the standard
correction, not a knob.

**Nothing else was changed after any learner result was seen, and no threshold
in §5 has been touched since this file was committed.**

## 8. What a pass licenses, stated in advance

A RECOVERED cell licenses exactly one sentence: *this learner can recover this
mechanism at this signal-to-noise on a panel of this size.* It licenses no claim
about real markets, no promotion, no lane, and no money. A FALSE-POSITIVE in
I, J-net or L disqualifies that learner from Phase 6 real-market work regardless
of how many other cells it recovered.
