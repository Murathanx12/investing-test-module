# FREEZE — the cross-sectional search phase is closed (2026-07-28)

**Cumulative candidates at freeze: 158.** Registry `TRIALS/registry.jsonl`,
42 registered trials and instruments. This note is binding: the search for new
cross-sectional stock-selection signals on public structured data is **closed**,
and reopening it requires the named exemptions in §5, not a good idea.

---

## 1. The binding sentence (B3, stated in full, not paraphrased)

The search **converged** at a cumulative count of **158**, which is **below the
~196 threshold** round 5 set as the next legitimate ceiling re-check. The
existing `INSTR-OVERFIT-CEILING` zero-skill envelope remains the operative alarm:

| H0 (zero information) | Expected max t |
|---|---|
| N = 53 signals, honest direction | **3.59** |
| N = 106 with sign flips | **3.98** |

Measured against that envelope, what motivated mining actually produced on our
own library: honest-direction best-of **t = 2.94–3.27** — *below* the zero-skill
expected max, i.e. a "best signal of the library" full-sample t≈3 is literally
indistinguishable from selection noise. With sign flips, **t = 6.16–6.58 at
Sharpe 1.44**, a fundable-looking track record built from zero information, and
the split-half check does **not** catch it (t > 3 in both halves).

**Stopping before the alarm threshold requires no new registration.** And the
reason it must not be reached artificially: **inventing filler hypotheses to
climb from 158 to 196 would be the exact self-deception the ceiling exists to
measure.** The ceiling is not a quota. Freezing at 158 on the existing
measurement is the correct action, and it is taken deliberately here.

## 3. Round 12's own record: 2 registered, 2 rejected, both by their control arm

| Trial | Treatment looked like | Control said |
|---|---|---|
| **TRIAL-EVENT-8K-FILTER** | Arm B cleared its frozen bar: −5.95% 3m, **t −7.06** | −12mo pseudo-event control, registered "expected ~0", returned **−6.79%, t −11.33** — *larger than the treatment* |
| **TRIAL-TEXT-LAZY** | Fallback trigger fired at **t −15.03** | Same filings scored against a **random different firm** fired it too, **t_NW −8.39**, 80% of treatment magnitude |

Neither opened its confirm window. Two for two in a single round is not bad
luck — it is the house discovering that its treatment effects were mostly
**design**. Both mechanisms are now named: 8-K = eligibility conditioning on
survival-to-event (distressed names leave the liquid universe *before* they
file); TEXT-LAZY = the bottom decile of any document-similarity score is a
persistent characteristic (82–89% the same names month over month), not an event
set.

**New house rule from this round, binding on all future registrations:** every
pre-declared fallback trigger must name **its own control arm and its own
overlap correction at registration time.** A fallback is a hypothesis; it does
not get to skip the design the primary hypothesis had to pass. Without the
diagnostic, TEXT-LAZY would have "failed into" a successor registration on a
statistic that pure noise satisfies at t = −8.4 — and eventually spent held-out
window on it.

## 6. The stopping argument, in one paragraph

Our own zero-skill envelope says a best-of-library t≈3 is noise. Our best
confirmed survivor sits at **t 1.11–1.45**. Chen & Velikov put the average
anomaly's net expected return at **8 bps/month** after effective spreads,
post-publication decay, and the post-2000 era. McLean & Pontiff put decay at
**58%** post-publication and find it *worse* for predictors concentrated in
liquid stocks — which is every candidate we ran. Our own `INSTR-CZ-CALIB` found
the rank correlation between published t-stat and in-window performance is
**−0.544**: on our data, the more celebrated the published finding, the deader
the signal. And Morningstar's decade barometer says **10%** of US large-cap
active funds survived *and* beat their average passive rival — among full-time
professionals with terminals, tick data, prime brokerage, and the ability to
short. Continuing to mine the same axis has near-zero expected yield, and every
additional pass raises the deflation count that every surviving claim must clear.
**Freeze.**
