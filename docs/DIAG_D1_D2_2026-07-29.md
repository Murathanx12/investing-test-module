## ADDENDUM (not pre-registered — context the two verdicts require)

**A1. 56 of 283 belief states retrieve fewer than 50 analogs; the minimum is 4.**
The guard in `retrieve_analogs` checks *candidate count* (`len(cand) < n`), not
*acceptance count* — with 21-td NMS, a candidate pool of C days can yield at most
~C/21 accepted analogs. All 56 short states are 2003-02-28 → 2007-11-30 (early
library). Those states are also where the young-analog mass sits: pooled share ≤12m
is 8.5% among the 227 full-50 states vs 28.3% among the 56 short states. This is a
silent degradation — the ledger records `state_probs` computed off as few as 4
analogs with no field marking the count.

**A2. The state_probs barely move off the unconditional base rate.**
Base rate over all 6,053 library days: fwd6m_positive 0.783, fwd12m_positive 0.854,
crash12m_dd15 0.356, crash12m_dd20 0.148.

| state_prob | mean abs deviation of 15-D belief from base rate | sd across the 283 states | mean abs Δ 15-D vs PC-3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| fwd6m_positive | 0.059 | 0.073 | 0.043 |
| fwd12m_positive | 0.070 | 0.085 | 0.028 |
| crash12m_dd15 | 0.125 | 0.129 | 0.036 |
| crash12m_dd20 | 0.085 | 0.087 | 0.024 |

Read together with the D2 table: **swapping the 15-D metric for a 3-PC metric
changes ~80% of the retrieved analogs (86.9% for 2-PC; Jaccard 0.070/0.110
converts to true set-change shares of 86.9%/80.2% for 50-element sets — noted
post-verification) but moves the published probabilities by less
than half of the (already small) distance between those probabilities and the
unconditional base rate.** The retrieval step is close to a no-op on the output.
That is a defect distinct from — and larger than — the dimensionality finding, and
it means the D2 kill line's stated remedy ("the 2–3-PC version is the honest
engine") does not follow from these numbers: the 2–3-PC engine is not measurably
better-informed, it is near-identically uninformative.
