## 1. Two measured facts that change the targets (derived BEFORE the grid)

Both are arithmetic on already-banked artifacts, not new results. They are
recorded here because each one invalidates an acceptance target that the
ratified proposal implied, and pre-registering an unreachable target would
manufacture a fake FAIL.

**(a) An I2 edge does not exist in the confirm window.** The I2 decay weight
is `w_m = exp(−m/τ)/mean(w)`, τ=60m, over the full 276-month panel. Mean
weight in the explore window (2004-01..2018-12) is **1.003**; in the confirm
window (2019-01..2024-12) it is **0.092**. A τ=60m edge has decayed to ~9% of
its explore-window strength before the confirm read starts. An α=0.4 I2 edge
is an α≈0.037 edge in the confirm window — below any honest detector.
**Therefore:** killing an I2 candidate at confirm is CORRECT behaviour, not a
false kill. End-to-end adoption power on I2 is not a legitimate target for
any ladder. I2 keeps the headline role for the EXPLORE-stage target and for
the posterior map (its conditioning world is "edges decay like
McLean-Pontiff"); **I1, the constant edge, becomes the primary power design**
— it is the only design whose edge still exists where the confirm gate looks.

**(b) DSR ≥ 0.95 on a 72-month single-signal book is unreachable by
construction.** With `sr_variance` floored at 0.01 and a 72-month confirm
window, `expected_max_sharpe` is 0.2209 (n_trials=42) / 0.2729 (n=179), so
DSR ≥ 0.95 requires an annualized net Sharpe of **1.47** (n=42) or **1.66**
(n=179). The measured explore book of a TRUE α=0.6 edge has t_net = 0.105
over 180 months → **SR_ann ≈ 0.03**. The gap is two orders of magnitude, and
turnover engineering is worth ~0.1–0.2 SR. No single-signal decile long book
in this pipeline can clear DSR@0.95 — this is the third killer M1 measured
(3 survivors at a0.6/I1, all 3 dsr_fail) and it is structural, not marginal.
**Therefore:** `dsr_threshold` is a CALIBRATED slot in the family below, not
an inherited constant. If the selected ladder lands at a threshold near 0,
DSR has become a reported diagnostic rather than a gate, and the verdict must
say so plainly — that is a real weakening of the multiple-testing defence,
and the FDR measurement plus PBO are what carry the load instead.

## 2. The ladder family (candidate BRAIN-009)

Fixed for every member: explore is gated on **t_ic only** (t_net recorded as
a diagnostic everywhere), the top-N cap ranks by t_ic, N=5, contaminated
signals never qualify, confirm keeps the IC sign gate (KILL if mean IC ≤ 0)
and drops the net-excess sign gate, PBO < 0.5, DSR deflated at the production
n_trials = 179.

Free parameters (`calibration/select.py:family()`, 600 members):

| slot | values |
|---|---|
| `explore_t_ic` | 1.25, 1.5, 1.75, 2.0, 2.25 |
| `explore_segments` | (largemid,) · (largemid, small) |
| `confirm_t_ic` | 0.5, 0.75, 1.0, 1.25, 1.5 |
| book (confirm + DSR) | `prod` (30% hold band) · `eng` (50% hold band) |
| `dsr_threshold` | 0.0, 0.10, 0.25, 0.50, 0.75, 0.95 |

`eng` is the implementation/turnover layer: the hold band widens so
incumbents are kept longer and the 25 bps cost is paid less often. **Costs
are not a knob** — `cost_bps_one_way` stays at the production 25 bps in both
books; only the trading rule moves.

The both-segments variant is the candidate fix for I3 structural blindness
(M1: 0 graduations at every α because explore scans largemid only). It costs
no extra compute — the small scans already run for the perf matrix — and it
does not inflate the deflation count, which is already 42 (21 signals × 2
segments).

## 3. The evidence bank — why the grid runs once

M1 cost ~170 core-seconds per cell and ~95% of that is the 42 explore scans,
which do not depend on the gate. `calibration/bank.py` banks the sufficient
statistics for the entire family (explore t_net/t_ic/contaminated/months for
all 42 scans; the injected candidate's confirm read in BOTH segments and BOTH
books, summary + monthly excess-net series; CSCV PBO; DSR of each confirm
book at n_trials 42 and 179; empirical and floored sr_variance). Every ladder
is then a pure function over the bank (`ruleset.evaluate`), so the family
search and its held-out validation cost zero additional compute.

Extra cost of the generality: 4 confirm scans per cell instead of ≤1 → 46
scans where M1 ran 42, about +9%.

**The bank is only trustworthy if it reproduces the control arm.**
`ruleset.BRAIN_008` is a byte-faithful replica of the frozen ladder, and
`scripts/verify_recal_bank.py` replays rep 0's a0.0/base and a0.6/I1 cells
through the bank and asserts the terminal state, explore t-stats, confirm
t-stats and sr_var match `rep_0000.json` / `rep_w2_0000.json` exactly. That
check must PASS before the grid launches. `tests/test_recal_ruleset.py`
pins the same equivalence at unit level (24 tests).

## 4. Selection protocol (pre-registered, binding)

- **Split:** SELECTION = even rep numbers, HELD-OUT = odd. A rep is the
  atomic unit (CRN pairs live inside a rep), so the split cannot leak.
  n = 125 per half at the registered 250 reps.
- **Feasible:** point FDR ≤ 0.05 **and** Wilson-95 upper ≤ 0.08, where FDR =
  P(adopt | α=0) in the `a0.0/base` cell, on the SELECTION half.
- **Objective:** maximize P(adopt | α=0.4, I1) on the SELECTION half.
- **Tie-breaks, in order:** P(adopt | α=0.2, I1) desc → FDR asc → strictest
  surviving ladder (dsr_threshold desc, explore_t_ic desc, confirm_t_ic desc,
  largemid-only before both-segments, prod book before eng). Ties resolve
  toward conceding as little as possible.
- **The winner is frozen** to `runs/GATE-M1/brain009_frozen.json` — that file
  IS the freeze record — and is then read ONCE on the HELD-OUT half. The
  held-out numbers are the reportable operating characteristics. If held-out
  FDR exceeds the budget, the ladder FAILS and does not ship, whatever the
  selection half said.
- Iterating the family (adding slots or values) requires a committed spec
  delta before the next selection run. No silent widening.
- Fitting thresholds against the simulator is calibration, not snooping:
  ground truth is known and no real data is touched. The one-shot real
  replay (§8) happens only after the freeze.

## 10. Spec delta — PBO becomes a calibrated slot (2026-08-06, pre-grid)

**Disclosure of source:** this delta is written before any RECAL-1 bank file
exists. The observation comes from `scripts/verify_recal_bank.py`, which
re-ran rep 0's `a0.0/base` and `a0.6/I1` cells purely to prove the bank
reproduces the BRAIN-008 control arm (it does: terminal state, explore
t-stats, confirm t-stats and sr_var all match `rep_0000.json` /
`rep_w2_0000.json` exactly). Those are control-arm cells already reported in
the M1 verdict, not treatment results.

**What it showed.** Two numbers that were never binding under BRAIN-008,
because nothing ever reached them:

1. **PBO is a property of the batch, not the candidate.** CSCV PBO is
   computed over the cell's 42 explore books, 41 of which are pure nulls, so
   the in-sample-best column is almost always a null and PBO sits near the
   coin-flip line: measured **0.514** (α=0 cell) and **0.586** (α=0.6/I1
   cell — a cell containing a strong TRUE edge). A fixed `PBO < 0.5` gate
   would therefore reject roughly half of all candidates for reasons
   unrelated to the candidate, halving power while buying FDR control that
   the IC stack already provides more cheaply. `pbo_threshold` joins the
   family as a calibrated slot — {0.5, 0.6, 1.0}, where 1.0 means PBO is
   computed and reported but does not gate — and the strictness tie-break
   prefers 0.5, so it only loosens if the frontier pays for it. Family size
   600 → 1800.
2. **The engineered book is worth ~0.24 annualized Sharpe, and it lifts the
   null too.** Turnover falls 0.134 → 0.090 one-way per month; the confirm
   book's annualized excess Sharpe moves −0.081 → +0.194 on the α=0 cell and
   +0.127 → +0.365 on the α=0.6/I1 cell. This confirms §1(b) from the other
   direction: turnover engineering is real but small, and it shifts null and
   true books together, so it cannot by itself make DSR@0.95 reachable. It
   also means the engineered book must be judged by the measured FDR, not
   assumed safe — which is what the α=0 cell is for.

No other slot changes. Any further widening needs its own dated delta,
committed before the selection run that uses it.

## 11. PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTION (written before the grid ran)

Recorded so the result can embarrass it. Parametric forecast from the
measured explore t_ic moments (`threshold_sweep.json`: mean −0.081 / 0.796 /
1.667 / 2.524 at α = 0 / 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.6, sd ≈ 1.0), with confirm-window
t_ic scaled by √(72/180) = 0.632 (constant edge, I1), explore and confirm
noise treated as independent, DSR and PBO inert:

| explore t_ic | confirm t_ic | FDR | Wilson hi (n=125) | power α=0.4 | power α=0.6 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.25 | 0.50 | 2.8% | 7.4% | **47%** | 78% |
| 1.50 | 0.50 | 1.8% | 5.9% | 40% | 73% |
| 1.50 | 0.75 | 1.3% | 5.2% | 35% | 68% |
| 2.00 | 0.50 | 0.6% | 4.0% | 26% | 60% |

**The call:** A1 (FDR ≤ 5%) and A2 (power ≥ 30% at α=0.4/I1) are jointly
achievable, and selection should land near explore t_ic ∈ [1.25, 1.5] with
confirm t_ic ∈ [0.5, 0.75], giving **FDR 1-3% and power 35-47%**. The 50%
stretch sits just outside the Wilson bar (e=1.25/c=0.25 predicts 52% power
at FDR 3.7%, Wilson hi 8.6% — infeasible by 0.6pp), so expect the stretch to
be missed narrowly and by the confidence rule rather than by the point
estimate.

Two ways this prediction is conservative, and one way it is optimistic:
- Conservative: the moments come from the I2 design. I1's monthly ICs are
  homogeneous where I2's are front-loaded, so I1's t_ic should be ≥ I2's.
- Conservative: explore and confirm t_ic share the true-edge component, so
  real correlation is ≥ 0; independence understates joint survival.
- Optimistic: it assumes DSR and PBO end up inert. If the selection lands on
  a gating DSR or PBO < 0.5, multiply the power column by that stage's pass
  rate — which for PBO is ≈ 0.5 by coin flip (§10).

If the measured operating point misses A2 badly while this forecast says it
should not, the gap is itself the finding — most likely in the cap, the
confirm sign gate, or an I1/I2 moment difference — and the verdict must
chase it rather than restate the target as failed.
